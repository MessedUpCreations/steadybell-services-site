Common questions

Before you forward a single call.

We build the call flow with you first, so SteadyBell knows what to say, what to collect, and when something needs your attention.

Do I have to change my business phone number?

No. The plan is built around forwarding calls you cannot answer while keeping the number your customers already know.

Will callers know they reached an answering service?

We use the greeting and business information you approve so the experience feels consistent with your company. We do not make claims or promises you have not authorized.

What happens if another call comes in while you are already on one?

Simultaneous-call handling depends on the phone setup and available staffing. Your fallback rules are established during onboarding so a busy moment does not become a mystery.

Do you answer nights and weekends?

Not as guaranteed live coverage at launch. Standard live answering is Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM Central, with your approved after-hours fallback outside that window.

What counts as receptionist time?

Live call duration plus the immediate post-call logging, handoff, and any authorized callback handling. Standard plans are billed in 30-second increments.
